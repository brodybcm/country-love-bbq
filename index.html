Hahira's Best-Kept Secret

Good Food,
Good People

Country Love Soulfood & BBQ started as a labor of love — a way to share generations of Southern cooking traditions with the community around Hahira, Georgia.

Open on weekends only, we keep things fresh, personal, and delicious. Every dish is cooked the old-fashioned way: from scratch, with quality ingredients, and plenty of love in every bite.

We're a local staple that Valdosta residents drive to Hahira just to experience. Once you try our oxtail or sweet potato pie, you'll understand why.
